Privacy Policy — JLPT Study

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information JLPT Study ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The App is a Japanese-language study tool. You may use an anonymous account or optionally create a permanent account with email, Google, or Apple to restore progress across installations and devices.

Information We Collect

Data typeWhat it isWhy it's collected
Anonymous identifier A random ID generated by Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in). No name, email, or password is attached to it. Lets study progress sync while you use the App without registration. Access may be lost after uninstalling unless you link a permanent account.
Optional account information Email address or basic Google/Apple account information, only when you choose to create or link a permanent account. Authenticates you and restores synchronized progress after reinstalling or changing devices.
Study progress & app activity Quiz scores, streaks, XP, vocabulary review history, and app settings (language, level, notification preferences). Core app functionality — saving your learning progress locally and in Firestore under your Firebase account.
AI grammar-practice submissions Japanese sentences you submit for grammar feedback, plus the selected grammar point. Sent to Google's Gemini API to evaluate that practice sentence. Used only when you request AI feedback.
Advertising identifier Your device's advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID), collected only if you have consented to personalized ads. Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure in-app ads.
Crash & diagnostic data Crash logs, stack traces, and basic device/OS information collected automatically by Firebase Crashlytics. Used to identify and fix bugs and crashes.

If you create a permanent account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address or the basic account information supplied by Google or Apple. We do not collect your phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other device content. The App does not request camera, microphone, location, or storage permissions.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used to display ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Gemini API — evaluates sentences submitted through grammar practice. Those sentences are processed by this service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Firebase processes account and synchronized study data to provide authentication and cloud storage. Google AdMob processes your advertising identifier for ad serving and measurement only as permitted by your consent choices.

Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can decline personalized ads via the in-app consent prompt shown on first launch (in supported regions), or through your device's ad settings.
  • Data deletion: Uninstalling removes locally stored data but does not delete Firestore records. Permanent-account users can delete their account and all synchronized study data from Settings → Account & cloud sync. Anonymous users can reset synchronized learning progress in Settings before uninstalling.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Security

Data transmitted to and stored in our backend (Firebase) is protected using industry-standard security practices provided by Google Cloud/Firebase, including encryption in transit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
