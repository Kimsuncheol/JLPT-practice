Privacy Policy — JLPT Study

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information JLPT Study ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The App is a Japanese-language study tool (vocabulary, kana, and quiz practice) and does not require you to create an account with a name, email address, or phone number.

Information We Collect

Data typeWhat it isWhy it's collected
Anonymous identifier A random ID generated by Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in). No name, email, or password is attached to it. Lets your study progress sync across app sessions and, if you reinstall on the same device, resume where you left off.
Study progress & app activity Quiz scores, streaks, XP, vocabulary review history, and app settings (language, level, notification preferences). Core app functionality — saving your learning progress, both locally on-device and synced to our database (Firestore) under your anonymous ID.
AI grammar-practice submissions Japanese sentences you submit for grammar feedback, plus the selected grammar point. Sent to Google's Gemini API to evaluate that practice sentence. Used only when you request AI feedback.
Advertising identifier Your device's advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID), collected only if you have consented to personalized ads. Used by Google AdMob to serve and measure in-app ads.
Crash & diagnostic data Crash logs, stack traces, and basic device/OS information collected automatically by Firebase Crashlytics. Used to identify and fix bugs and crashes.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personally identifying information. The App does not request camera, microphone, location, or storage permissions.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used to display ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Gemini API — evaluates sentences submitted through grammar practice. Those sentences are processed by this service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is your device's advertising identifier, shared with Google AdMob for ad serving and measurement, and only if you have consented to personalized advertising. Your anonymous Firebase ID and study progress are not shared with third parties.

Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can decline personalized ads via the in-app consent prompt shown on first launch (in supported regions), or through your device's ad settings.
  • Data deletion: Uninstalling the App removes all data stored locally on your device. Because accounts are anonymous and not linked to any personal identifier, we cannot look up or delete a specific person's cloud-synced data on request unless you contact us with your in-app anonymous ID. To request deletion of your synced progress data, contact us using the details below with your anonymous ID (found in the app's settings screen, if available) or simply uninstall the app to stop further syncing.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Security

Data transmitted to and stored in our backend (Firebase) is protected using industry-standard security practices provided by Google Cloud/Firebase, including encryption in transit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
